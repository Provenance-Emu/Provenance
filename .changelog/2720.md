### Added
- **Face-Cam Overlay** — `PVCameraOverlayView` displays `RPScreenRecorder.cameraPreviewLayer` as a configurable picture-in-picture overlay during ReplayKit recordings (iOS only, Provenance Plus).
- **Camera Overlay Settings** — Position (4 corners), size (small/medium/large), and shape (circle/rounded rect) are configurable in Recording & Streaming settings.
- **Live preview of settings changes** — The overlay repositions and resizes immediately when the user changes settings, without restarting the recording.

### Changed
- **Recording Settings UI** — Camera section expanded to show overlay position, size, and shape pickers when face-cam is enabled; live streaming section updated to mention upcoming direct RTMP support.
