### Added
- **MFi+ HUD Indicators** — Added `analogMode` and `swapMode` indicator IDs to the `PVIndicatorRegistry` system, with predefined state enums `PVAnalogModeIndicatorState` and `PVSwapModeIndicatorState`.
- **PSX Analog Mode Indicator** — The PSX Analog toggle now shows a persistent green dot in the HUD overlay when analog sticks are active, and hides when in digital mode.
