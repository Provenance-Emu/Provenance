### Added
- **TV Bezels in Portrait** — On iPhone and iPad a TV now shows around the picture in portrait as well, drawn close so the picture has nearly the whole width, with the controls over the console under it.

### Fixed
- **Portrait TV Under the Notch** — A portrait TV started at the very top of the screen, under the notch or Dynamic Island; it now starts below it.
- **Bezel Picked While Playing Did Not Show** — A TV chosen from the pause menu's list could stay hidden behind the game's own TV or its Bezel Project artwork; the TV picked is now the TV shown.
- **Bezel Change Needed a Restart** — After one TV failed to load, no other built-in TV would load until the game was restarted or the device rotated.
