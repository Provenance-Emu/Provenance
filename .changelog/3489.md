### Added
- **AU Audio Effects Chain** — Post-process emulator audio with built-in Apple Audio Units: reverb, delay/echo, distortion, low/high/band-pass filters, parametric EQ, peak limiter, and dynamics processor.
- **Effects Presets** — Save and load named snapshots of your effects chain; four factory presets included (Room Reverb, Retro Echo, Warm, Lo-Fi).
- **Audio Effects Settings UI** — New "Audio Effects" page under Settings → Audio Engine for building and managing effects chains.
- **Live Reload** — Effects chain changes apply immediately to a running emulator session without restarting audio.
