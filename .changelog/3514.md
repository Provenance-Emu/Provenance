### Added
- **Multi-Select Mode** — Tap "Select" in the game library title bar to enter multi-select mode; game tiles show circle badges and a bottom toolbar tracks the selection count.
- **Normalize Titles Batch Action** — Select multiple ROMs and tap "Normalize Titles" to preview and apply display-name cleanup (region tags, revision tags, disc markers, GoodTools brackets, trailing article fix) in one step.
- **`ROMTitleNormalizer`** — Pure, testable utility shared with the forthcoming import-time normalization feature.
