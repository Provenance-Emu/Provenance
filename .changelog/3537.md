### Fixed
- **PVRetroArch Mupen64Plus-Next rumble regression** — iOS 26+ overwrote the mupen64plus-next `.opt` file on every launch, clearing user-set pak types back to `"memory"` and silencing rumble-pak games (GoldenEye, Star Fox 64). The overwrite now preserves existing `mupen64plus-pak*` settings. Fresh installs default `pak1` to `"rumble"` so rumble-pak games work out of the box pending per-game auto-detection (#3129).
