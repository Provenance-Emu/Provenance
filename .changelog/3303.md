### Added
- **PVAppIntents module** — New Swift Package providing `GameEntity`, `SystemEntity`, `SaveStateEntity` AppEntities and five modern `AppIntents` (`LaunchGameIntent`, `ToggleFavoriteIntent`, `ListRecentGamesIntent`, `PlayRandomGameIntent`, `GetLibraryStatsIntent`) with `AppShortcutsProvider` Siri phrases. Replaces the legacy `INIntent`-based shortcuts with a type-safe, iOS 17+ entity graph usable by the main app, widget extensions, and Siri.
