### Added
- **Physical Case Controller Detection** — `CaseControllerDetector` identifies connected iPhone cases with built-in buttons by `GCController.vendorName` (smart cases: GameSir Pocket Taco, Soolra) and by companion skin identifiers published on deltastyles.com (passive cases: Buppin, which has no Bluetooth). Posts `PVPhysicalCaseDidConnect`, `PVPhysicalCaseDidDisconnect`, and `PVPhysicalCaseSkinDetected` notifications; shows a toast when a known case is connected
- **`PhysicalCaseLayout` model** — stores case name, MFi vendor strings, button count, and known skin identifiers per supported case
- **`autoLoadCaseSkin` setting** — new `Defaults.Keys` entry (iOS/macOS only) for enabling or disabling auto-skin application when a physical case is detected
