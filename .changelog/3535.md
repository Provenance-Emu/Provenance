### Changed
- **PVFeatureFlags: thread-safe synchronous reads** — Removed `@MainActor` isolation from `PVFeatureFlags`. All `isEnabled` and subscript reads are now synchronous and callable from any thread or actor without `await`. Internal state is protected by `NSLock` with a pre-computed `[String: Bool]` cache rebuilt atomically on config changes. `PVFeatureFlagsManager` now subscribes to a `stateDidChange` Combine publisher instead of requiring manual `updateFeatureStates()` calls at every mutation site. Added `PVFeatureFlags.shared[.feature]` subscript shorthand.
