### Added
- **DualSense / DS4 light bar theming** — DualSense and DualShock 4 light bars now change color to match the current system
- **DualSense microphone button mute** — Pressing the DualSense microphone button during gameplay now toggles game audio on or off
- **Controller light bar settings** — New DualSense and DualShock 4 light bar and microphone button options in Controller settings
- **Share and Create button remapping** — DualSense Create and DualShock 4 Share buttons can now be remapped to any in-game action
- **Touchpad click remapping** — DualSense and DualShock 4 touchpad click can now be remapped like other buttons
- **Light bar master switch** — New master switch to turn controller light bar colors on or off
- **Per-system light bar colors** — Option to customize controller light bar colors for each system
- **Custom mic button behavior** — Option to choose what the DualSense microphone button does during gameplay
