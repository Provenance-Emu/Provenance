### Fixed
- **On-screen controller touch routing** — `PassThroughView` (indicator overlay) now uses explicit subview iteration in `hitTest` with a `hit !== subview` guard, ensuring the container never claims a touch for itself. Touches in dead-zone areas always fall through to the game-control views (JSDPad, JSButton) beneath the overlay. Fixes all plist-based UIKit controller buttons (d-pad, face buttons, shoulder buttons, start/select) being unresponsive after the keyboard/mouse/JIT overlay additions from PRs #3061 and related.
