### Added
- **Recording & Streaming Settings** — New `RecordingSettingsView` in Settings providing per-preference control over screen recording behaviour: microphone enable/disable, camera toggle (iOS only), auto-save to Photos, HUD recording button visibility, and clip duration selector (15 s / 30 s / 60 s).
- **`Defaults+Recording.swift`** — Five new `Defaults.Keys` in `PVSettings`: `recordingMicEnabled`, `recordingCameraEnabled`, `recordingAutoSave`, `showRecordingOSD`, and `recordingClipDuration`. Added `recordingCameraPosition` key for camera overlay corner.
- **`CameraPosition`** — New `CameraPosition` enum in `PVSettings` with four cases (`topLeft`, `topRight`, `bottomLeft`, `bottomRight`) for selecting the camera preview overlay corner. Picker shown in Recording & Streaming settings when camera is enabled.
- **`PVRecordingManager`** — `startRecording()` now reads `Defaults[.recordingMicEnabled]` instead of always enabling the microphone, so the user preference is respected at recording start.
- **Settings navigation** — "Recording & Streaming" section wired into the Emulation tab on iOS (collapsible layout).
