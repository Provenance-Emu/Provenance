### Added
- **`CameraPosition`** — New `CameraPosition` enum in `PVSettings` with four cases (`topLeft`, `topRight`, `bottomLeft`, `bottomRight`) for selecting the camera preview overlay corner during screen recording.
- **`recordingCameraPosition` key** — New `Defaults.Keys.recordingCameraPosition` in `PVSettings` (default: `.bottomRight`) persisting the chosen camera overlay corner across sessions.
- **Camera Position picker** — Picker shown in Recording & Streaming settings (iOS only) when the camera toggle is enabled, letting users choose which screen corner displays the face-cam overlay.
