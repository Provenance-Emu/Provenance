### Added
- **Siri Prediction Donations** — Provenance now donates an `NSUserActivity` with `isEligibleForPrediction = true` on every game launch, teaching Siri Suggestions to surface recently and regularly played games on the Lock Screen and in Spotlight based on time-of-day patterns.
- **AppState play-activity tracking** — `AppState.currentPlayActivity` holds a strong reference to the active prediction activity for the duration of a game session; setting it to `nil` on game exit automatically calls `resignCurrent()` to record a clean session end-time for Siri learning.
