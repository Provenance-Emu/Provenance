### Added
- **Auto-translation pipeline** — `Scripts/auto_translate.py` and `.github/workflows/auto-translate.yml` detect new/changed English strings and auto-translate them to 11 languages (zh-Hans, ja, ko, es, pt-BR, de, fr, it, nl, ru, ar) using Claude Haiku, opening a draft PR for admin review. Only translates delta keys for cost efficiency (<$0.01/run).
