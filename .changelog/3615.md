### Added
- **PVLogFileManager** — `PVLogging` module now supports writing rotating, date-stamped session log files to `Library/Logs/Provenance/`. Up to 10 files, 2 MB each, managed automatically.
- **Session Logs Browser** — New Settings › Debug › Session Logs screen to view, share, and delete session log files; shows logging on/off status with a toggle.
