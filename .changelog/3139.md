### Added
- **Animated skin backgrounds** — Orientation representations in `info.json` can now declare a `backgroundAnimation` block with `type` (frames/apng/gif), `frames` (array of filenames), `fps`, and `loops`. The new `DeltaSkinBackgroundAnimation` model is decoded by `OrientationRepresentations`. `DeltaSkinView` renders a `DeltaSkinAnimatedBackgroundView` beneath the game screen when a background animation is configured. Frame-sequence animations are driven by a `TimelineView` and pause when the app is backgrounded; `.apng` and `.gif` types display the first frame
