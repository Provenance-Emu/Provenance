### Changed
- **Cheats Menu Finds Cheats by Itself** — Opening the cheats menu for a game now lists your saved cheats together with the cheats known for that game from the built-in database and from online sources, which are fetched in the background. Select a cheat to switch it on; nothing is switched on for you. The separate search and download screen is gone, and Refresh checks online again.

### Fixed
- **Online Cheats Hidden for a Day After Opening Offline** — A cheat lookup made with no network was remembered as "no cheats" for 24 hours.
