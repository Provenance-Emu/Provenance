### Added
- **SNES Multitap (Mednafen)** — CRC32-based detection enables 5-player (and 8-player homebrew) multitap for snes_faust and bsnes modules; game list mirrors snes9x
- **Saturn TeamTap (Mednafen)** — Serial-based detection enables 6-player TeamTap for known Saturn Bomberman and sports titles
- **Genesis/Mega Drive TeamPlayer** — ROM peripheral header detection automatically enables 4-player TeamPlayer for Mega Drive games that declare multitap support in the ROM header
