### Changed
- **SwiftUI Side Menu** — Replaced UIKit `SideNavigationController` with `PVSwiftUISideMenuContainer`, a `UIViewController` that hosts a pure-SwiftUI animated side-menu overlay driven by spring animations and `PVRootViewModel.isMenuVisible`. Adds left-edge pan gesture (iOS) to open the menu and tap-to-dismiss / drag-to-close on the dimming overlay. Menu width remains orientation-aware (30% iPad, 70% iPhone portrait, 40% iPhone landscape). Removes the `SideNavigationController`, `SideNavigationController+NestedTypes`, `UIViewController+SideNavigationController`, and `SideMenuNavigationController` source files.
