### Added
- **Reproducible dylib builds** — `cores.yml` now has a `pinned_date` field that locks all RetroArch buildbot dylib downloads to a specific nightly snapshot, preventing silent upstream regressions and core-version churn across CI/developer machines
- **`update-dylib-pins.sh`** — new helper script to bump the pinned buildbot date in `cores.yml` (`./update-dylib-pins.sh` for today, or `./update-dylib-pins.sh YYYY-MM-DD` for a specific date)

### Changed
- **`get-modules.sh`** — reads `pinned_date` from `cores.yml` and substitutes it for `latest` in all buildbot URLs; falls back to `latest` when no pin is set
