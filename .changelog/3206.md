### Added
- **Light Gun Input via Mouse/Touch** — New `LightGunResponder` protocol in `PVCoreBridge` lets cores declare light gun support; `GCMouseLightGunDriver` accumulates GCMouse delta events (USB/Bluetooth HID mice, iPadOS pointer) into a normalised crosshair coordinate and drives the responder.
- **Thin libretro light gun bridge** — `PVThinLibretroFrontend` now handles `RETRO_DEVICE_LIGHTGUN` in its `thin_input_state` callback, exposing screen-X/Y, trigger, reload, aux-A/B, start, select, and d-pad queries; NES/SNES/Genesis/PSX/Saturn/MAME systems are pre-registered as light gun capable.
- **Light gun mouse sensitivity setting** — `PVSettings.lightGunMouseSensitivity` (0.1–5.0, default 1.0) scales GCMouse delta accumulation; stored in UserDefaults.
