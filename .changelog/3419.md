### Added
- **Atari 7800 ROM header normalization** — `A7800HeaderDetector` detects and skips the 128-byte `.a78` header so headered ROMs match No-Intro/OpenVGDB MD5 hashes on import.
- **Atari Lynx ROM header normalization** — `LynxHeaderDetector` detects and skips the 64-byte `.lnx` header so headered ROMs match No-Intro/OpenVGDB MD5 hashes on import.
