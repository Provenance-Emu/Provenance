### Added
- **SNES9x LightGunResponder** — Super Scope and Konami Justifier light gun support for the native SNES9x core. Aim, trigger, reload, and auxiliary buttons (Cursor, Pause, ToggleTurbo) are all mapped via the `LightGunResponder` protocol, enabling touch/GCMouse pointer input for games like Yoshi's Safari, Super Scope 6, and Lethal Enforcers.
