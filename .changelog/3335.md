### Fixed
- **Flycast (Dreamcast) Mouse Input** — Flycast no longer boots with mouse active for standard games; port 0 is explicitly set to RETRO_DEVICE_JOYPAD for non-mouse titles, and RETRO_DEVICE_MOUSE for confirmed mouse-compatible games (Typing of the Dead, Planet Ring, Floigan Brothers), ensuring touch/pointer events reach the Dreamcast Maple bus mouse peripheral correctly
