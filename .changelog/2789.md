### Fixed
- **Recent Saves autosave flooding** — The "Recent Saves" section in the Game Library now shows at most one autosave per game (the most recent) by default, preventing timed/session autosaves from flooding the strip and hiding saves from other games. Manual saves are always shown.

### Added
- **"Show All Auto-Saves in Recent Saves" setting** — A new toggle in Settings → Appearance lets power users opt in to see every autosave in the Recent Saves carousel. Default is off (collapsed: one auto-save per game).
