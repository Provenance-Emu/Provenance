### Fixed
- **Recent Saves autosave flooding** — The "Recent Saves" section in the Game Library now shows at most one autosave per game (the most recent) by default, preventing timed/session autosaves from flooding the strip and hiding saves from other games. Manual saves are always shown.

### Added
- **Per-game stacked autosaves** — All autosaves for a game are visually grouped behind a single representative card. The newest save is shown on top; ghost cards and a "+N" badge indicate hidden depth. Time-gap dividers ≥ 2 h are shown as session-break indicators inside the filmstrip.
- **Filmstrip autosave timeline** — Long-press (or tap the "+N" badge, or use the context menu) on a stacked autosave card to expand a full-screen filmstrip showing every autosave for that game. Time gaps between saves are shown inline, with session-break highlights for gaps over 2 hours.
- **"Show All Auto-Saves in Recent Saves" setting** — A new toggle in Settings → Appearance lets power users opt in to see every autosave individually in both the Home strip and RetroWave/tvOS shelves, bypassing the stacking behaviour. Default is off (stacked).
