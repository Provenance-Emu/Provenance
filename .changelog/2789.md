### Fixed
- **Recent Saves autosave flooding** — The "Recent Saves" section in the Game Library now shows at most one autosave per game (the most recent) by default, preventing timed/session autosaves from flooding the strip and hiding saves from other games. Manual saves are always shown.

### Added
- **Session-stacked autosaves** — Autosaves from the same gaming session (within 2 hours of each other) are visually grouped as a card "stack". The newest save is shown on top; ghost cards and a "+N" badge indicate hidden depth.
- **Filmstrip session timeline** — Long-press (or tap the "+N" badge, or use the context menu) on a stacked autosave card to expand a full-screen filmstrip showing every save in that session. Time gaps between saves are shown inline, and large gaps (> 1 h) are highlighted as session breaks.
- **"Show All Auto-Saves in Recent Saves" setting** — A new toggle in Settings → Appearance lets power users opt in to see every autosave individually, bypassing the stacking behaviour. Default is off (stacked).
