### Added
- **ArtworkMatchingService** — New protocol-driven service (`ArtworkMatchingServiceProtocol` / `ArtworkMatchingService`) that encapsulates the progressive-fallback artwork search logic previously duplicated across `ArtworkSearchQueue` and `BatchArtworkMatchingView`.
- **Box back artwork at import** — `ArtworkSearchQueue` now requests both `.boxFront` and `.boxBack` in the primary pass and persists the back-cover image to `game.boxBackArtworkURL` when TheGamesDB returns one.
- **Background artwork tasks** — Screenshot and title-screen artwork are fetched in a `.background`-priority `Task` after box art is saved, keeping the import pipeline non-blocking.
