### Added
- **Controller preferred player-slot** — `PVControllerManager` now reads a `UserDefaults`-backed preferred player slot (1–8) per controller (keyed by `vendorName`) and honours it when the controller connects or reconnects, instead of always assigning to the next-available slot. Public API: `preferredPlayer(for:)`, `setPreferredPlayer(_:for:)`, `saveCurrentPlayerSlotAsPreference(for:)`.
