### Added

- **MFi+ modifier swap-modes — Saturn, 32X, GameCube, Jaguar** — Extends controller mappings to expose missing inputs on GCGamepad/extended gamepad profiles:
  - **Saturn (Mednafen, GCGamepad)**: Hold L1+R1 modifier — DPad Up → L trigger, DPad Down → R trigger, A → Start. D-pad Up/Down and shoulder buttons (C/Z) suppressed while modifier is active to prevent double-fire.
  - **32X (PicoDrive, GCGamepad)**: New GCGamepad mapping section — A/B/C/X/Y/Z mapped to face+shoulder buttons; hold L1+R1 combo adds Start (mod+A) and Mode (mod+B).
  - **GameCube (Dolphin)**: Hold both triggers simultaneously in GC mode fires BUTTON_Z; trigger axes are suppressed on both sides during the combo to avoid accidental L/R presses.
  - **Jaguar (RetroArch)**: Numpad buttons 1–4 mapped to L3/R3/buttonOptions/buttonHome on the touch controller; 5–9 and 0 are no-ops pending additional RetroArch core support.
