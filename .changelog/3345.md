### Fixed
- **Flycast 30FPS Support** — Dreamcast games that render at 30fps (one frame per two vblanks) are now detected automatically. The libretro core measures the dupe-frame ratio over a 120-frame window and adjusts the reported FPS via `RETRO_ENVIRONMENT_SET_SYSTEM_AV_INFO`, so Provenance's emulation loop syncs at 30fps instead of 60fps. Based on upstream flyinghead/flycast commit fb69bd8.
