### Changed
- **SwiftUI scroll performance** — Reduced scroll stutter: view identities in `ConsoleGamesView` now use `absoluteString` instead of string-interpolated `Optional(...)`, ordered refresh in `TVMediaLibraryModel` prioritises the visible system, `loadAllGames` uses `defer` to guarantee the loading state resets on cancellation, and `CustomPageIndicator` centres indicators without a `ScrollView` when all fit in the viewport.
