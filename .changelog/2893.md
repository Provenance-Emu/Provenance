### Added
- **Hardware Switch UI** — Physical-looking toggle switches for Atari 2600 (left/right difficulty A/B) and Atari 7800 (left/right difficulty) now appear in both the default SwiftUI skin and the legacy UIKit controller overlay, dynamically shown only for systems that have hardware switches. Includes a new `HardwareSwitchView` / `HardwareSwitchRowView` SwiftUI component with retrowave neon styling and haptic feedback on iOS; switch descriptors are sourced from `SystemIdentifier.hardwareSwitches`
