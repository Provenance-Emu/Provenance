### Added
- **Hardware Switch UI** — Physical-looking toggle switches for Atari 2600 (left/right difficulty A/B) and Atari 7800 (left/right difficulty) now appear in both the default SwiftUI skin and the legacy UIKit controller overlay, dynamically shown only for systems that have hardware switches. Includes a new `HardwareSwitchView` / `HardwareSwitchRowView` SwiftUI component with retrowave neon styling, haptic feedback on iOS, and a `hardwareSwitches(for:)` helper that maps system identifiers to their switch descriptors
