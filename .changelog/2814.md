### Added
- **Genesis Plus GX: EA 4-Way Play multitap support** — Automatically detects known EA Sports 4-Way Play titles (FIFA International Soccer, FIFA Soccer 95/96/97, NBA Live 95/96, Madden NFL 96, Bill Walsh College Football) and configures the EA 4-Way Play adapter (`SYSTEM_WAYPLAY` on both controller ports), enabling up to 4 simultaneous players. Sega TeamPlayer detection remains unchanged and continues to use `SYSTEM_TEAMPLAYER` on port A
