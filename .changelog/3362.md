### Added
- **SNES Mouse support in PVSnes9x native core** — `PVSNES9xEmulatorCore` now implements `MouseResponder`; games detected by the existing CRC32 list (Mario Paint, Mario & Wario, Arkanoid, Dezaemon, and 70+ others) automatically activate the virtual trackpad overlay and forward touch/pointer events as SNES Mouse input on port 1.
