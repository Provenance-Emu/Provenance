### Changed
- **Network Play offers every game** — Any downloaded game whose system has a core that supports network play can be hosted or joined. The "Unverified" list, the multiplayer details editor and the play-style filter are gone.
- **Network Play settings are a tab** — Settings sit beside Games and Rooms on the Network Play screen instead of opening as a separate screen.

### Fixed
- **Network Play focus on Apple TV** — Buttons and filter chips on the Network Play screens use the same focus highlight and theme colours as the rest of the app.
