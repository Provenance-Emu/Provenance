### Added
- **RetroArch Light Gun** — `PVRetroArchCoreCore` (thick RetroArch wrapper) now conforms to `LightGunResponder`, enabling GCMouse and touch-gesture light gun input for all RetroArch-hosted cores that declare `RETRO_DEVICE_LIGHTGUN` (NES Zapper, SNES Super Scope/Justifier, PSX GunCon, Saturn Stunner, Arcade/FBNeo, MAME, etc.).
