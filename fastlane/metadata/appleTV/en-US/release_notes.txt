Provenance comes to Apple TV.

This is the first release of Provenance for tvOS — the multi-system retro emulator, now on the big screen.

• Play games from over 60 retro systems on your Apple TV
• Nintendo, Sega, Sony, Atari, Neo Geo, PC Engine, arcade (MAME), DOS and more
• Sega Dreamcast via a new Flycast-based core, and Atari Jaguar CD with HLE BIOS
• Game controller support: MFi, Xbox, PlayStation and custom button mapping
• Save states, a built-in cheat database and RetroAchievements
• iCloud sync brings your library, save states and BIOS files over from iPhone and iPad (Provenance Plus)

Import your own ROM and BIOS files to get started.
